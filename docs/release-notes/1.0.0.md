### What's new

- ClipDeck keeps your entire clipboard history with no limit. Search as you type, and paste items with their original formatting.
- Press Shift-Command-C to show your history in a Liquid Glass panel over whatever you're doing without taking focus from your app.
- Click the paper clip in the menu bar, then click an item to copy it.
- Choose a maximum storage size, and ClipDeck removes the oldest items automatically.
